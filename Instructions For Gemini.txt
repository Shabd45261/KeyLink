App Idea:

Name-KeyLink

Purpose- Keyboard , Mouse trackpad for Windows in one app

Sides- pc side application (helps pc connects to android) and android side application(main keyboard and trackpad app)

Layout- 

1. App opens up and then the user shows a menu of recent connected devices with green arrows and red arrows (green for online devices) (red for offline devices)
2. The user clicks on one of the online device and then it connects to the pc side client . Note that the pc will be shown as online if the user has started the server on pc side
3. On connecting to the pc a keyboard will be open in landscape fullscreen mode as per the image
4. there are two keypad buttons at the left most bottom part and right bottom most part of the keyboard which will on clicking will open the fullscreen potrait keypad profile
